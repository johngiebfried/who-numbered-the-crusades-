# Nine, then eight: How Wikipedia Numbered the Crusades

Final scroll story of the Wikipedia sub-project (9 November 2026). Live version: Claude dashboard artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/NHzfxkeMeY3BocN1EEZZi8 (private until shared).

- `standalone.html` opens in any browser (data and images embedded; uses `d3.min.js` and `dash-shim.js` in this folder).
- `index.html`, `story.css`, `story.js`, `charts.js`: the page as stored in the dashboard.
- `data/`: the datasets. Hand-coded: `eras.csv`, `events.csv`, `holyland.csv`, `absences.csv`. Derived: `wp_counts.csv` (Wikipedia rows of `data/ledger/ledger.csv`), `scope_grid.csv` (from `data/wikipedia/scope/box_membership_matrix.tsv`). Images are base64 WebP of public-domain Wikimedia Commons files.

Structure: Opening (the 2001 article, Schaff-Herzog, 9/11, edits, the 2004 box); Act I numbers; Act II the English box; Act III 22 languages; Coda the Tenth.
