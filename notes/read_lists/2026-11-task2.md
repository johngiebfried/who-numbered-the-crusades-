# Pages for the author to read himself (task 2, Strand B)

Ranked by how much a published claim would rest on them. None of these could be read as text in this session (Gallica, Google Books text and MDZ were closed; archive.org text opened only when a search returned the text URL).

1. **Lefranc, *Histoire du moyen âge*, 3e éd. (Lyon/Paris: Périsse, 1838) and 7e éd. (1841), crusade chapters and table des matières.** Europeana record for 1841: https://www.europeana.eu/item/04202/BibliographicResource_3000135712252 . Check whether slots four to six still read Henry VI 4, 1202–04 5, Frederick II 6 (as in 1834), or have moved. If they moved, this is the school-market route to Bouillet.
2. **Koch, *Tableau des révolutions de l'Europe*, Paris: F. Schoell, 1807, t. III "Tablettes chronologiques" (years 1195–1270) and t. I Période IV.** Gallica: https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k1042963h ; BSB: urn:nbn:de:bvb:12-bsb11690731-2 (t. 1); Google Books IcpNAAAAcAAJ (t. 1, Tablettes from p. lxxi). Decides whether C 4, D 5, F 6, L1 7 "last" is Koch's own (in his lifetime) or Schöll's 1814/1823 revision.
3. **Koch, *Tableau des révolutions de l'Europe dans le moyen âge* (Lausanne/Strasbourg: Bauer, 1771), crusade paragraph.** https://archive.org/details/tableaudesrvolu07kochgoog . Same question, one generation earlier.
4. **Koch–Schöll, nouvelle édition (Paris, 1814), t. 1, Période IV** (Gallica has t. 2 bpt6k9815643d and t. 3 bpt6k9816855m; locate t. 1). French wording of "septième et dernière"; does the 1814 text already have it?
5. **Valentin, *Abrégé de l'histoire des croisades (1095–1291)* (Tours: Mame, 1841), table des matières.** https://archive.org/details/abrgdelhistoire00valegoog . A crusade schoolbook of 1841 that must number; Catholic school market (Bibliothèque de la jeunesse chrétienne).
6. **Le Bas, *France: Annales historiques* (Didot, L'Univers, 1840–43), t. 1, years 1195–1270.** Look for the chronological table later reused under CROISADES in *France: dictionnaire encyclopédique* t. 6 (1842).
7. **Des Michels, *Précis de l'histoire du moyen âge*, 3e éd. (Paris: Colas, 1832) and any later Hachette edition**, crusade section. The Université's standard précis.
8. **Michaud and Poujoulat, *Histoire des croisades abrégée à l'usage de la jeunesse*, 1st ed. 1838, title page and table.** The 1886 Mame printing (https://archive.org/stream/histoiredescrois00mich/histoiredescrois00mich_djvu.txt , table at the end) has the Michaud scheme; confirm the 1838 text is the same.
9. **Buchon, *Chronique de la conquête de Constantinople et de l'établissement des Français en Morée* (1825), introduction.** https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_OlscpMdCp-0C . Does Buchon call 1202–04 "la quatrième croisade" in 1825, as he does in 1840–41?
10. **Université: programme of history questions for the baccalauréat ès lettres (1840) and the collège history programmes of the 1830s.** Not located online. The protocol's central hypothesis rests on these.
