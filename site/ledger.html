How the ledger was made

Printed works come from the project's numbering corpus (the November 2026 review), hand-coded into expedition codes. A blank cell means the source as checked gives no number for that expedition; it is not a claim that the source is silent. Rows resting on a heading or one passage are as reliable as that passage. Two rows (EB9, Newman) record only the ends of a nine. The early modern rows give totals only.

Wikipedia rows are the crusade navigation box sampled on 1 July each year, collapsed into stretches in which its numbering did not change; the last stretch runs to November 2026. "Named" there means the box links the expedition without a number. The modern list used for coloring is C 4, D 5, F 6, L1 7, L2 8; Henry VI's crusade, the Barons' Crusade and Edward's have no number in it.
