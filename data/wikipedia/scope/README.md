# Crusade side boxes: scope across languages

`box_membership_matrix.tsv`: one row per expedition (Wikidata item) linked from a crusade side box in any of 22 Wikipedias; one column per language. A cell is the year the item first entered that language's box; `2018x21` means it entered in 2018 and was gone by 2021. Two boxes in one language (fr, ar, tr) are merged. Retrieved 9 November 2026 (SHA-256 prefix 03f28a34ecbaae62).

Categories: NUM numbered Levant crusades (incl. Edward's), LEV other Levant expeditions, POP popular crusades, IBER Iberia, NORTH Baltic and northern, HER heretics, POLIT political crusades against Christians, LATE after 1291, PROTO "proto-crusades", MODERN post-1789 wars. Battles, people, states and institutions are left out of the matrix.
