How Wikipedia numbers the crusades, 2001–2026

The highest crusade number each page uses, as the page stood on 1 July of each year, in ten language editions. German Wikipedia has kept its own seven since 2004; the others settled on nine, Arabic last (2011–13), and English dropped back to eight after its Ninth Crusade was renamed in 2019.
